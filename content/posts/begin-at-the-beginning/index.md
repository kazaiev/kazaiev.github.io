OxidizedInk 0.1.0 is out. All 32 steps on the roadmap are done, from reading the first EPUB to the book's own styles, and it's a real release now: a changelog, tests on every pull request, and a firmware image you can flash.

It has been a fun journey, and I learned a thing or two about the Rust ecosystem.
