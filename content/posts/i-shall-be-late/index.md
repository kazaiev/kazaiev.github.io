I reprinted the case in translucent smoked PETG. So far so good. I'm doing some user testing – and can't say I miss anything.

![The reader in its new case of translucent smoked PETG, three buttons down the right side, lying beside a PCB ruler. The screen shows a page of Neuromancer, with the chapter, THREE, at the bottom left and the battery at 80%, page 3/21 and 15% at the bottom right](smoked-petg.jpg)

My wife laughs that instead of just reading *Neuromancer* for our family book club, like a normal person, I started this project specifically to have an excuse not to finish the book on time. "It's compiling!"
